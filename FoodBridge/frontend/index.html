How FoodBridge Works

Three simple steps to make an impact

1. List Surplus Food

Donors (restaurants, hotels, canteens) list available surplus food with details like quantity, type, and pickup location.

2. Request & Approve

Verified NGOs browse available food, request what they need, and donors approve requests based on availability.

3. Collect & Distribute

NGOs collect approved food from donors and distribute to beneficiaries. Track completion and impact.

Key Features

Verified NGOs
